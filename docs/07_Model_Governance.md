# Model governance and limitations

Before deployment, document data provenance, target definition, training window, sector coverage, sampling, performance by class, threshold rationale, and model owner. Use time-aware out-of-sample validation; random splits can overstate performance in financial panels.

Do not use the screener as the sole basis for credit, investment, employment, or legal decisions. Review restatements, liquidity facilities, covenant headroom, auditor commentary, off-balance-sheet exposures, macro conditions, and sector-specific metrics. Reassess thresholds after material economic or accounting changes.
